# Management commentary

## FY2025

USCS revenue exceeded AOP by $345.0 million, supported by lending and fee growth. Provision was $133 million favorable. Higher rewards and services cost of $580.2 million and higher salaries and other operating expense of $280.7 million more than offset those benefits, leaving pretax income $389.9 million below plan.

## Q2 2026 latest estimate

H1 2026 revenue reached $18,647 million and pretax income reached $3,821 million. The FY2026 latest estimate is $38,370.1 million of revenue and $7,894.1 million of pretax income. Lower provision and marketing partly offset higher rewards, benefits and operating expense.

## Risks and opportunities

The main downside combines slower billed business with higher credit costs. Acquisition reallocation and earlier automation savings provide the largest controllable upside. These items remain outside the latest estimate until their evidence thresholds are met.

# 2025 AOP backtest

## Outcome

The frozen AOP planned $34,469.0 million of revenue and $7,199.9 million of pretax income. Public actuals were $34,814 million and $6,810 million. Pretax income missed plan by $389.9 million.

## What drove the result

- Revenue beat plan by $345.0 million. Stronger lending and fee revenue offset a modest billed business miss against the AOP.
- Provision was $133 million favorable as losses and reserve needs were below the original plan.
- Rewards, business development and Card Member services were $580.2 million unfavorable. Published commentary links the increase to billed business, rewards, partner payments, benefit usage and new U.S. Platinum benefits.
- Salaries, employee benefits and other operating expenses were $280.7 million unfavorable, reflecting higher allocated service and compensation costs.

## Forecast learning

After Q2 2025, the first-half run rate supported a higher rewards and services forecast. Credit performance partly offset that expense variance. Reporting revenue, provision and controllable expense separately would have shown the underlying cost trend.
